01 / THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

Customer churn is a business signal,
not just a data point.

A retail bank wants to reduce customer attrition. The challenge is to use historical customer information to identify patterns associated with account closure and help the retention team focus its attention.

The dataset contains 10,000 customer records covering credit score, geography, age, tenure, account balance, estimated salary, product count, credit-card ownership and account activity.

The target field is Exited: a value of 1 indicates that the customer has closed their account. The project builds a classification workflow around this target.

↗

Project objective — move from raw customer records to model-generated churn predictions and a report that helps explore customer attrition patterns.

Set up the Fabric environment

Created a Microsoft Fabric workspace and the FabricData_Sciencelakehouse Lakehouse to hold the source, prepared data and model predictions.

02

Ingested and explored customer data

DATA PREPARATION

Installed the required library, downloaded the churn CSV into Lakehouse files, and explored the dataset using Spark, pandas, seaborn and Matplotlib.

PythonPySparkpandasSeaborn
03

Cleaned the data and engineered features

FEATURE ENGINEERING

Used Data Wrangler to remove duplicate and incomplete rows and exclude non-predictive identifier fields. Created five additional features and one-hot encoded categorical data.

NewTenureNewCreditsScoreNewAgeScore NewBalanceScoreNewEstSalaryScore
04

Prepared balanced training data

MODEL INPUT

Split the dataset into training and test sets, encoded categorical variables, aligned test features with the training columns, and applied SMOTE to balance the training data.

scikit-learnOne-hot encodingSMOTE
05

Trained and tracked three ML models

EXPERIMENTATION

Trained two Random Forest classifiers with different maximum depths and one LightGBM classifier. Used MLflow experiment tracking to log runs, parameters and metrics, and registered the models.

MODEL 02Random ForestMax depth 8

06

Evaluated models and saved predictions

EVALUATION

Loaded the registered models, generated predictions against the held-out test set and compared the results using labeled confusion matrices. Saved actual and predicted labels in the df_pred_results Delta table.

Confusion matrixClassification reportDelta Lake

Delivered the Power BI churn report
